# Formation Mode

Unlocked automatically after Survival Wave 3 is cleared.

## Play area

- Players operate in a zone near the bottom of the screen.
- Movement is horizontal only (left/right).
- No screen wrapping, rotation, thrust, or inertia.
- Movement is immediate and arcade-like.

## Controls

### Player 1

- **A** — move left
- **D** — move right

### Player 2

- **Left arrow** — move left
- **Right arrow** — move right

## Visuals

- Dark purple background with a slowly scrolling starfield (upward travel illusion).
- "Formation Mode" label in the top-left corner.
- Player zone boundary line above the player area.

## Placeholder content

Until formations are implemented, five static placeholder enemies appear near the top of the screen.

## Not yet implemented

- Enemy formations and group movement
- Enemy bombs
